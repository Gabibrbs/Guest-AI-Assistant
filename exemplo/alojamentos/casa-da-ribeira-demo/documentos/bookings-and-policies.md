# Bookings and policies

## Payment
- Bookings made through a booking platform are paid on that platform.
- Direct bookings: 30% when you book, the rest 7 days before arrival, by bank transfer or card.
- There is no security deposit.

## Cancellation
- Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival.
- Between 14 and 7 days before arrival: 50% refund.
- Less than 7 days before arrival, or no-show: no refund.
- If you booked on a platform, that platform's cancellation policy applies.

## Tourist tax
The city of Porto charges a tourist tax of €3 per guest per night, up to 7 nights. Children under 13 do not pay. The tax is not included in the price and is paid to the host on arrival, by card or cash.

## Invoices
Invoices can be issued in the name of a person or a company. Send the name, address and tax number to the host by email before check-out. The invoice is sent by email within 5 working days.

## Minimum stay
2 nights. 3 nights on New Year's Eve and during the São João festival in June.
