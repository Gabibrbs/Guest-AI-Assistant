# The house

Casa da Ribeira Example is a one-bedroom apartment for up to 4 guests: a double bed in the bedroom and a sofa bed in the living room.

## Wi-Fi
- Network: CasaRibeira_Guest
- Password: azulejo2026
- If the Wi-Fi stops working, unplug the white router in the living room cabinet, wait 30 seconds and plug it in again.

## Air conditioning and heating
There is one unit in the bedroom and one in the living room. Both do cooling and heating. Use the remote control on the wall. Please turn them off when you go out and keep the windows closed while they are on.

## Kitchen
- Fridge with small freezer, induction hob (2 plates), oven, microwave, kettle, toaster and a capsule coffee machine.
- Some capsules, tea, sugar, salt, olive oil and washing-up liquid are provided for the first days.
- Pots, pans, plates, glasses and cutlery for 4 people.
- The dishwasher tablets are under the sink.

## Washing machine
In the kitchen. Detergent is in the cupboard above it. There is a drying rack behind the bathroom door. There is no tumble dryer.

## Bed linen and towels
- Beds are made when you arrive.
- Each guest has one large and one small towel.
- For stays of 7 nights or more, linen and towels are changed once for free.
- Extra towels: €5 per set, ask the host.

## Rubbish and recycling
Separate the rubbish into:
- yellow bag: plastic and metal;
- blue bag: paper and cardboard;
- green bag: glass;
- black bag: everything else.

The recycling bins are at the end of the street, about 50 metres to the left when you leave the building.
