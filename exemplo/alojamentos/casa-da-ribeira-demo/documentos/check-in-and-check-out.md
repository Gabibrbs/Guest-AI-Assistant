# Check-in and check-out

## Times
- Check-in: from 15:00 to 22:00.
- Check-out: by 11:00.
- Early check-in or late check-out may be possible if the apartment is free. Ask the host at least one day before. Late check-out until 13:00 costs €20.

## How to get in
The apartment is on the 2nd floor. There is no reception: you enter on your own with a key box.

1. The street door opens with the code you receive by message on the day of arrival.
2. Next to the apartment door there is a small grey key box. Use the same code to open it.
3. Take the key and close the box again.

The code changes for every booking. Please do not share it.

## Late check-in (after 22:00)
Arrivals after 22:00 are possible but must be agreed with the host in advance. There is no extra charge until midnight. After midnight there is a €15 fee.

## Luggage
- You can leave your bags in the apartment hallway on the day of arrival from 12:00, if the cleaning is finished. Ask the host first.
- On the day of departure, bags can stay in the hallway until 15:00.

## Before you leave
- Leave the key inside the key box and scramble the code.
- Put used towels in the bathroom.
- Take the rubbish out (see "The house").
- Close the windows and turn off the air conditioning.
