# The neighbourhood

All places below are within walking distance. Distances are approximate.

## Shopping
- **Minimercado Azul** (mini-market): 2 minutes. Open every day from 08:00 to 22:00. Bread, fruit, drinks and basics.
- **Supermercado Ponte Velha** (larger supermarket): 10 minutes. Open every day from 08:00 to 21:00.

## Pharmacy
- **Farmácia do Largo Novo:** 4 minutes. Open Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 20:00.
- On Sundays and at night, the pharmacy door shows which pharmacy is on duty.

## Food and drink
- **Tasca da Vó Rosa:** 3 minutes. Traditional Portuguese food at local prices. Closed on Mondays.
- **Café Gaivota:** next door. Breakfast, pastries and coffee from 07:30.
- **Cais 14:** 6 minutes, by the river. Grilled fish and a terrace with a view. Booking recommended at weekends.
- **Pizzaria Três Arcos:** 7 minutes. Good for families. Open until midnight.

## Cash machine
There is a cash machine (ATM) 3 minutes away, on the corner of the square.

## Things to see
- The river and its wine cellars across the bridge: 10 minutes.
- Viewpoint over the old town: 12 minutes uphill.
