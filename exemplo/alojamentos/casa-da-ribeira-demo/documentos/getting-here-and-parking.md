# Getting here and parking

The apartment is in the historic centre of Porto, a short walk from the river. You will receive the exact address after booking.

## From the airport
- **Metro:** take the metro from the airport to the city centre (about 35 minutes), then walk about 10 minutes downhill to the apartment. A single ticket costs around €2.50.
- **Taxi or ride app:** about 25 minutes, usually between €20 and €30 depending on traffic and time of day.

## From the train stations
- **São Bento station:** about 8 minutes on foot.
- **Campanhã station** (long-distance trains): take a local train or the metro to São Bento (5 minutes), then walk.

## Parking
- The street in front of the building is pedestrian-only. Cars can stop for a few minutes to unload luggage.
- The nearest public car park is about 5 minutes on foot. It is open 24 hours and costs about €18 per day.
- Street parking in the centre is limited and paid during the day.

## Getting around
Most sights are within 20 minutes on foot. The streets are steep and paved with stones, so comfortable shoes help.
