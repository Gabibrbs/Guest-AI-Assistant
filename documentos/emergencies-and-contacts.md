# Emergencies and contacts

## In an emergency
Call **112** (free, works from any phone). It is the European number for police, fire brigade and ambulance. Operators speak English.

## If something stops working
- **No electricity:** the fuse box is behind the front door, at the top. Turn up any switch that is down. If it goes down again, unplug the last appliance you turned on and call the host.
- **No hot water:** the water heater is in the kitchen cupboard next to the fridge. Check that the small blue light is on. If it is off, press the reset button once and wait 5 minutes.
- **Water leak:** close the main tap under the kitchen sink (turn it clockwise) and call the host.
- **Locked out:** call the host. A spare key can be brought in about 30 minutes.
- **Lost key:** a replacement costs €40.
