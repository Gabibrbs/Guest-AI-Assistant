# House rules

- **Maximum guests:** 4 people. Only guests in the booking can stay overnight.
- **Visitors:** day visitors are allowed until 22:00. Please tell the host in advance.
- **Quiet hours:** from 22:00 to 08:00. The building has neighbours who live here all year.
- **Parties and events:** not allowed.
- **Smoking:** not allowed inside the apartment, including vaping. You can smoke on the street. Smoking inside has a €100 cleaning fee.
- **Pets:** small pets (cats and small dogs) are welcome with a €15 cleaning fee per stay. Please tell the host when you book.
- **Shoes:** please do not wear outdoor shoes on the sofa bed.
- **Damage:** if something breaks, tell the host. Accidents happen and small things are not charged.
